# Code of conduct

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other discussions hosted by this project. Contributors and users are welcome regardless of background, identity, or experience.

## Expected behavior

Discuss the code and the problem. Be respectful when you disagree, accept corrections, and give others room to learn. Keep feedback specific and relevant to the work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

Report a concern to the maintainer, [JDeffner](https://github.com/JDeffner). If you do not have a private contact route, [open a contact request](https://github.com/JDeffner/px-toolkit-notepadpp/issues/new?title=Private%20maintainer%20contact%20request) without names or sensitive details and ask for one. GitHub's report-abuse tools are also available for content or conduct that violates GitHub's policies, including concerns involving the maintainer.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close a discussion, warn a participant, or restrict participation. They should explain moderation decisions when doing so does not disclose private information. Participants may request reconsideration with relevant context. Reports will be handled with care, but no response deadline is guaranteed.
